{{- /* Small square tiles over a list of photo ids, for places that want a sample of the library rather than a listing of it. The squares are F-5's 1:1 derivatives; a photograph too small to crop one keeps its own shape. The markup is trimmed hard because one page may hold the whole library (F-23): the tiles are the page. Whitespace is chomped, and `sizes` states the tile's real width rather than a share of the viewport. Both grids that use this partial are `.squares`, whose cells are minmax(132px, 1fr), so a tile is ~132-200px wide at every width above one column -- a viewport share made the browser fetch the 400px crop for a 155px box. */ -}} {{- $sizes := "(max-width: 300px) 100vw, 200px" -}} {{- /* Called with a plain list of ids, or with a dict carrying that list, a "more" cell to close the grid with -- F-24 spends the last cell on the way to the rest rather than on one more photograph -- and a "scope", the listing these tiles belong to. With a scope each tile leads to the photograph inside that listing (F-11a), which carries its neighbours; without one, as on the front page and the All page, it leads to the photograph's own page (F-11). */ -}} {{- $ids := . -}} {{- $more := false -}} {{- $scope := "" -}} {{- if reflect.IsMap . -}} {{- $ids = .ids -}} {{- $more = .more -}} {{- with .scope }}{{ $scope = . }}{{ end -}} {{- end -}}
